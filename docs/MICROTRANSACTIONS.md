# Freemium Microtransactions Catalog

Lots of purchasable items to maximize revenue while keeping core free.

## Currencies
- **Coins** (soft): Earned by completing games, daily login, watching ads. Used for temporary unlocks, re-rolls.
- **Gems** (hard): Bought with real money. Used for permanent unlocks, cosmetics, energy, VIP.

## Energy System
Free users: 5 energy / day. 1 energy = 1 game.
- energy_5 : 5 energy for 50 gems
- energy_20 : 20 energy for 150 gems
- energy_50 : 50 energy for 300 gems
- energy_unlimited_24h : Unlimited for 24h - 200 gems or $1.99 IAP

## Subscriptions
- **VIP Monthly** ($4.99/mo): No ads, +2 daily energy, exclusive roles rotation, exclusive cosmetics, 100 gems/month, priority support
- **VIP Yearly** ($39.99/yr): Same + 2 months free + exclusive yearly badge
- **Battle Pass Season** ($9.99): Track progress, unlock exclusive skins, roles, gems over 30-60 days. Free track exists with limited rewards.

## Limited Time / Event Offers
- Holiday packs (Halloween Werewolf, Christmas Mafia) - special roles + themes bundle $4.99
- First purchase bonus x2 gems
- Daily deals rotating discounts
- "Starter Pack" one-time $1.99: 500 gems + energy + 1 premium role

## Ads (free tier)
- Rewarded video: +1 energy or 20 coins or temporary role try
- Interstitial after games (removable)

This catalog allows dozens of individual purchase points while core loop remains free and fun.
