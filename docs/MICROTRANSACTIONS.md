# Freemium Microtransactions Catalog — Mafia Themed

Everything is flavored for the underworld. Players feel like they’re running a crime family.

## Currencies
- **Cash** (soft currency): Earned by finishing jobs (games), daily payoffs, and watching “street intel” ads. Used for temporary favors, re-rolls, and small bribes.
- **Diamonds** (hard currency): Bought with real money. Used for permanent power, exclusive roles, energy, and status.

## Hits (Energy System)
Free crews get **5 Hits per day**. Each game costs 1 Hit.
- energy_5  → 5 Hits for 50 Diamonds (“Quick reload”)
- energy_20 → 20 Hits for 150 Diamonds (“Crew restock”)
- energy_50 → 50 Hits for 300 Diamonds (“Full arsenal”)
- energy_unlimited_24h → Unlimited Hits for 24h — 200 Diamonds or $1.99 (“All-night operation”)

## Subscriptions & Status
- **Made Man Monthly** ($4.99/mo)  
  No ads • +2 daily Hits • exclusive rotating roles • exclusive cosmetics • 100 Diamonds every month • priority “family” support

- **Made Man Yearly** ($39.99/yr)  
  Same benefits + 2 months free + exclusive yearly “Don” badge

- **Family Legacy Pass** (Battle Pass, $9.99)  
  Season-long track of exclusive skins, roles, Diamonds. Free track exists with smaller rewards.

This catalog keeps the core party game free while giving players plenty of underworld-flavored ways to spend.
