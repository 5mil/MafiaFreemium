# Freemium Microtransactions Catalog — Pure Mafia Style (No Ads)

**No ads at all.** The game is completely ad-free.  
Players only spend real money or Diamonds to get more power, reduce wait times, unlock content, and show status.

## Currencies
- **Cash** (soft currency): Earned purely by finishing jobs (games) and daily payoffs. Used for small temporary favors and re-rolls.
- **Diamonds** (hard currency): Bought with real money. The main way to unlock permanent power, skip waits, and buy status.

## Hits (Energy / Time Gate)
Free crews get **5 Hits per day**. Each game costs 1 Hit.  
This is the main “reduce time” pressure.

- **5 Hits** — 50 Diamonds (“Quick reload”)
- **20 Hits** — 150 Diamonds (“Crew restock”)
- **50 Hits** — 300 Diamonds (“Full arsenal”)
- **All-Night Operation** — Unlimited Hits for 24 hours (200 Diamonds or $1.99)

## Subscriptions (Status + Convenience)
- **Made Man Monthly** ($4.99/mo)  
  +2 daily Hits • exclusive rotating roles • exclusive cosmetics • 100 Diamonds every month • priority family support

- **Made Man Yearly** ($39.99/yr)  
  Same benefits + 2 months free + exclusive “Don” badge

- **Family Legacy Pass** ($9.99)  
  Season-long exclusive skins, roles, and Diamonds. Free track exists with smaller rewards.

### Design Philosophy
- Completely ad-free experience
- Core party game always free and fully playable
- Microtransactions only for:
  - More content (premium roles, cosmetics)
  - Reducing time gates (Hits / energy)
  - Status & convenience (Made Man, Legacy Pass)
- No pay-to-win in the actual social deduction gameplay — only cosmetic and convenience advantages

This keeps the game clean, respectful of players, and still highly monetizable through pure microtransactions.
